# 📄 Activity 7: Resume Generating

> *Comparing multiple AI models to generate, refine, and format a professional, ready-to-print resume.*

## 📖 About This Activity

In this activity, we tested and compared two different artificial intelligence models—**Gemini** and **Claude**—by tasking both with generating a resume. The goal was to evaluate how each model handles layout, structure, and content generation, and to refine the outputs through iterative prompting.

## 💡 What Have I Learned?

* **Key Concepts & Skills:** I learned that different AI models like Gemini and Claude produce distinct outputs, and comparing them helps identify which tool best fits a specific task. However, even the stronger output isn't completely perfect on the first try, requiring iterative prompting to specify what details to add or remove from the existing draft.

* **Challenges & Solutions:** While generating my resume in Gemini, it initially produced a plain, text-only document without any visual layout or styling instead of a ready-to-print file—so I refined my prompt to explicitly request a modern design formatted as a downloadable PDF.

* **Future Applications:** Moving forward, I can regularly update this resume as I gain new qualifications and training experiences, while also incorporating a professional photo of myself and organizing the layout even more cleanly.
