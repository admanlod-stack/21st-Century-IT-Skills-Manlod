# 👾 Activity 2: Personal Branding

> *Crafting a unique visual identity that reflects personal interests and stands out to an audience.*

## 📖 About This Activity

This activity focused on designing a personal brand strategy to shape how I present myself visually and stand apart from others. By developing my own brand identity, I explored how consistent visual elements make a personal profile more recognizable, authentic, and relatable to an audience.

## 💡 What Have I Learned?

* **Key Concepts & Skills:** I learned the fundamentals of building a personal brand from scratch, including how to curate aesthetically pleasing visual elements, establish a cohesive color scheme, and select matching typography.

* **Challenges & Solutions:** Defining what my brand should represent was initially challenging, so I reflected on my recent hobbies and drew inspiration from the video games I enjoy playing, which naturally led to a distinct, pixel-art-inspired theme.

* **Future Applications:** Moving forward, I plan to experiment with refined color palettes and more readable typography, as well as redesign my logo to make it even more eye-catching and memorable.
