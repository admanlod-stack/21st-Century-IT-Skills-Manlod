# 💻 Activity 4: Google Workspace Presentation: Google Forms & Google Meet

> *Exploring collaborative productivity tools, highlighting key features, and engaging audiences through interactive demonstrations.*

## 📖 About This Activity

In this group reporting activity, each team was assigned specific Google Workspace applications to present to the class. Our group focused on **Google Forms** and **Google Meet**, showcasing their core purposes, key features, and practical uses for everyday collaboration and productivity.

## 💡 What Have I Learned?

* **Key Concepts & Skills:** I gained a much deeper understanding of both Google Forms and Google Meet, discovering useful features and functionalities within each application that I had never used or known about before.

* **Challenges & Solutions:** During our presentation, a poor internet connection prevented us from running our planned interactive activity across both apps simultaneously, so we adapted on the spot and used Google Forms alone to host an engaging "Two Truths and a Lie" game with the class.

* **Future Applications:** In future presentations, we can improve our visual demonstrations by including more screenshots and clear images of specific app features, ensuring the audience can easily follow along even if live connectivity issues arise.
