# 🎨 Activity 1: Presentation Design Principles

> *Mastering visual hierarchy, typography, and layout structure to create impactful and readable presentations.*

---

## 📖 About This Activity
This activity focused on understanding and applying the foundational rules of effective slide design. By analyzing the differences between cluttered, poorly structured slides and clean, well-organized ones, this exercise emphasized how visual aesthetics work hand-in-hand with strong content to communicate ideas clearly to an audience.

---

## 💡 What Have I Learned?
* **Key Concepts & Skills:** I learned how to identify and apply core presentation design principles to distinguish well-structured slides from poorly designed ones, recognizing that effective communication requires both strong content and intentional visual design.
* **Challenges & Solutions:** Finding a font pairing that matched my design's theme was initially difficult, so I experimented with multiple options before combining a bold typeface with an italicized font that visually resembled steam rising from a coffee cup.
* **Future Applications:** Moving forward, I plan to download custom fonts outside of Canva's default library and incorporate more aesthetically pleasing visual elements that capture the audience's attention while keeping the text easy to read.
